# Chai stall breakdown — pilot (stock-first explainer)

Pilot for the stock-first explainer route (`visual: footage`, running `tally`).
Format modelled on business-breakdown explainers: one day at the cash box, every
rupee on screen, a twist, the month.

## Sources (checked 12 Nov 2026)
- Commercial LPG 19 kg, Hyderabad: ₹3,081 — Goodreturns, LPG price in Hyderabad (12 Nov 2026)
- Vijaya toned milk 1 L: ₹62 — BigBasket listing (undated retail; Hans India reported ₹58 after the last revision)
- Sugar, bulk: ₹45/kg — IndiaMart Hyderabad sugar listings (12 Nov 2026)
- Tea powder: ₹180/kg — Telangana Today, "Irani chai now costs Rs 25" (older report)

## Estimates (shown as estimates on screen)
Cup price ₹15, 400 cups/day, 60 ml milk + 2.5 g tea + 8 g sugar per cup, one
cylinder lasts 5 days, paper cup ₹0.60, helper ₹600/day, site rent ₹300/day.

## The arithmetic (every figure on screen comes from here)
| Item | Day |
|---|---|
| Milk 24 L × ₹62 | ₹1,488 |
| Tea powder 1 kg | ₹180 |
| Sugar 3.2 kg × ₹45 | ₹144 |
| Gas ₹3,081 / 5 days | ₹616 |
| Paper cups 400 × ₹0.60 | ₹240 |
| Materials (₹6.67 a cup) | ₹2,668 |
| Helper + rent | ₹900 |
| **Out** | **₹3,568** |
| **In** 400 × ₹15 | ₹6,000 |
| **Kept** | **₹2,432** (× 30 = ₹72,960 ≈ ₹73,000) |

Milk share 1,488 / 3,568 = 41.7% ≈ 42%. ₹2/L rise = 24 L × ₹2 × 30 = ₹1,440 a month.
