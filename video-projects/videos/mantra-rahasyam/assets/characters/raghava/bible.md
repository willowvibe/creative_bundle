# Raghava (Nanna): character bible

**Role:** Abhinav's father, keeper of the family's faith and of the Mahamrityunjaya mantra. Appears in every episode.
First appearance: Episode 1.

## Who he is
- **Age about 56.** A retired government employee (state electricity department): disciplined, simple, honest.
- **Quiet strength.** He jokes gently and worries silently. His love shows in small instructions ("ride slowly").
- **Faith from his father.** Tatayya taught him the Mahamrityunjaya mantra. In Ep1 he chants it, three cycles, at
  the moment of crisis, and that act brings his son back.
- **Proud father.** He doesn't say it often; when he does ("I'm proud of you"), it lands.
- **With Lakshmi:** calm where she is emotional. Calls her by name, "లక్ష్మీ".

## Look (keep identical in every episode)
- **Face:** lean face, kind but tired eyes, a calm, serious mouth that breaks into a warm smile.
- **Hair:** receding grey hairline, short and neatly combed back. A thick **grey moustache**; clean-shaven
  otherwise.
- **Skin:** medium-dark brown.
- **Build:** medium height, lean.
- **Signature items, every episode:** **three horizontal vibhuti stripes** on the forehead with a small red kumkum dot
  in the centre, **rudraksha mala** around his neck, steel-strap wristwatch on the left wrist.

## Wardrobe
| Context | Outfit |
|---|---|
| Home (Ep1 morning) | cream half-sleeve cotton shirt, white dhoti or beige trousers |
| Hospital (Ep1) | the same cream shirt, untucked, a cloth bag on his shoulder; holding the consent-form pen with a trembling hand |
| Chanting (Ep1) | sitting cross-legged or standing with eyes closed, palms joined, holding the rudraksha mala |
| Festival / temple | white shirt and dhoti with a silk angavastram |

## Story so far
- **Ep1:** reminds Abhi to ride slowly and wear Tatayya's rudraksha → signs the consent form with a trembling hand →
  remembers his father's words → chants the mantra three times → his son returns. He tells Abhi he's proud of him for
  saving the girl.
