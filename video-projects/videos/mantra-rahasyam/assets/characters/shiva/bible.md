# Lord Shiva (divine form): character bible

**Role:** the divine presence who answers the mantra. Recurs across the series. First appearance: Episode 1.

## Portrayal
- **Calm, compassionate, all-powerful.** Never angry on screen. Speaks briefly and with absolute authority.
- **Mostly light.** A radiant white-gold figure, often seen as a silhouette inside the light, with the face revealed
  only at key moments.
- **Iconography:** trident (trishul) with a small damaru, crescent moon in matted hair, Ganga as a fine stream of
  light from the hair, a serpent around the neck, third eye shown as a mark (never glowing red), blue-grey skin,
  tiger-skin garment, rudraksha.

- **Clothing:** a **tiger-skin** wrap over one shoulder and around the waist, tied with an **orange-red sash**.
- **Trishul** (silver-gold) with a small **damaru** tied with orange thread, held in his right hand.
- **Light:** a bright burst of white-gold energy and sparkles behind him. Bright and divine, never dark.
