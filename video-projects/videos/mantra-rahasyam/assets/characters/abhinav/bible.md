# Abhinav (Abhi): character bible

**Role:** hero of మంత్ర రహస్యం. Appears in every episode. First appearance: Episode 1.

## Who he is
- **Age 25.** Software engineer in Hyderabad who has just received his first salary (Ep1).
- **Only son** of Raghava and Lakshmi; the whole family's happiness rests on him.
- **Kind and selfless.** He acts before he thinks when someone is in danger: in Ep1 he swerves his bike to save a
  little girl, and that act is why Lord Shiva sends his soul back ("his work on earth is not done").
- **Devoted son and grandson.** Adored his late Tatayya. He wears Tatayya's rudraksha and wants to fulfil Tatayya's
  wish of taking the family to Srisailam.
- **Modern but rooted.** Jokes with his father and loves his mother's food, but takes off his shoes before the
  pooja room and touches his parents' feet before big days.

## Look (keep identical in every episode)
- **Face:** handsome, warm and approachable rather than tough; oval face with a defined jawline, expressive warm
  dark-brown eyes, straight nose, easy warm smile with even teeth.
- **Hair:** thick black hair, short on the sides, neatly styled with a soft side part.
- **Facial hair:** light, neatly trimmed stubble. Never a full beard, never clean-shaven.
- **Skin:** warm wheatish (medium brown) Telugu complexion, healthy glow.
- **Build:** slim and fit, about 5'10".
- **Signature item, every episode:** **Tatayya's rudraksha bracelet on his right wrist** (single row of brown beads
  on a red thread).

## Story so far
- **Ep1:** first-salary day → saves a girl and is hit by a lorry → surgery → his soul meets the Yama Dhoothas → Shiva

  a great responsibility that will give your life its meaning awaits you." This is the hook for Part 2.
