# Lakshmi (Amma): character bible

**Role:** Abhinav's mother, the emotional heart of the series. Appears in every episode. First appearance: Episode 1.

## Who she is
- **Age about 50.** Homemaker; the house runs on her love and her routines.
- **Deeply devout.** Lights the deepam every morning and talks to "శివయ్యా" as if he were family.
- **Loving and protective.** She feeds Abhi curd and sugar before every important day. She worries out loud, and
  cries easily but recovers fast.
- **Strong underneath.** In a crisis she prays without stopping: "take my life, save my child."
- **With Raghava:** a warm, teasing marriage of 28 years. She calls him "ఏమండీ".

## Look (keep identical in every episode)
- **Face:** soft, round, kind face; large dark eyes with fine lines; warm smile.
- **Hair:** long black hair with a few grey strands at the temples, centre parting, in a low neat bun. Fresh
  jasmine (మల్లెపూలు) in the bun at home and at festivals.
- **Skin:** medium brown, warm undertone.
- **Build:** average height, slightly plump, graceful.
- **Signature items, every episode:** small round red bindi, thin gold chain with a mangalsutram, green and gold
  glass bangles (two or three on each wrist), small gold stud earrings, kumkum in the parting.

## Wardrobe
| Context | Outfit |
|---|---|
| Home (Ep1 morning) | maroon cotton saree with a thin gold border, worn in the everyday Telugu style, matching blouse |
| Hospital (Ep1) | the same maroon saree, a little crumpled, pallu pulled over one shoulder; holding a small Shiva photo or a rudraksha |
| Festival / temple | silk saree (mustard or deep green with a broad gold border), jasmine in her hair |
