# Research — famous food by district, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana

Draft 2, 26 Nov 2026 (gap research done). Two kinds of pairing:

- **GI** — a registered Geographical Indication. Strongest claim; shown with a "GI" badge.
- **Known for** — a popular association (food press, tourism sites). Never presented as official.

`status`: ✅ sourced · 🟡 one weak source, owner to confirm · ❓ no source found, owner to supply.

## Recommendation for the Shorts

- **Short A (Andhra, ~10):** Tirupati laddu, Bandar laddu, pootharekulu, Kakinada kaja, Banaganapalle
  mango, Guntur mirchi, Araku coffee, Nellore chepala pulusu, Bhimavaram royyalu, Rayalaseema ragi
  sangati. Six GI badges carry the credibility.
- **Short B (Telangana, ~8–10):** haleem, biryani, Irani chai, Warangal chapata chilli, Armoor turmeric
  (GI applied), Balanagar custard apple (GI applied), sarva pindi, jonna rotte, Rajavva atukulu, sakinalu
  — once the owner confirms the 🟡 rows.
- Long version waits for the owner's ❓ fills plus comments from both Shorts.
