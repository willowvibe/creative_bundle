# Tiffin centre teardown — research

**Question:** what does a Hyderabad tiffin centre owner actually keep?

master + two helpers) from public prices checked on 2026-11-25. Estimates are labelled.

## City comparison — Hyderabad vs Chennai vs Bengaluru

Same centre, same menu, same 300 plates, same ingredient basket (rice, dal and oil trade at similar
south-Indian wholesale prices; the model holds them equal — a stated simplification). What changes
per city: gas, rent, staff, and the price customers pay. Licences held at ₹1,200/yr in all three
(city fees not researched; immaterial at ~₹3/day).

**Reading:** costs barely differ by city (about ₹640/day between cheapest and dearest). The price
customers accept differs by ₹15 a plate = ₹4,500 a day. At the same ₹40 plate Bengaluru keeps the
*least* (highest wages); it wins only because darshini customers pay ~₹55. Chennai edges
Hyderabad on lower wages and gas. So "where does it make more sense" is decided by what the street
will pay, not by what the shop costs — and the Bengaluru ₹55 is the number to verify first.
