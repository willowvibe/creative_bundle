## Hanja check

| Character | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 己 | Gi | Yin Earth |
| 卯 | Myo | Rabbit |
| 庚 | Gyeong | Yang Metal |
| 辰 | Jin | Dragon |

Hangul used for ideas the brief explains in Korean: 사주 (Saju), 절기 (solar
term), 태양 (sun). The Latin chips are dates, a year and a count — 1 JAN,
20 JAN, 2000, 1 of 8 — so they carry no reading risk.

## The two worked examples

Both computed with the engine, not invented:

- 20 January 2000 → year pillar **己卯**, Earth Rabbit. Before Ipchun, so the
  Saju year is still the one that began in February 1999.
- 6 February 2000 → year pillar **庚辰**, Metal Dragon. After Ipchun, which fell
  on 4 February 2000.

Seventeen days apart, same calendar year, different animals — which is the whole
argument of the video, and the comment-bait.

## Why the closing line is the CTA

Same shape as short 2: the last spoken line *is* the offer, and the end card
holds the URL for that whole line plus a 1.2 s tail. No "link in bio", no logo,
no second mention. "Free" is the only word that gets a pill.
