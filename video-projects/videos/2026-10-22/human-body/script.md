# What Is the Human Body Made Of?

**Format:** 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps, ~60 s
**Voice:** Sarvam Bulbul v4 Flash (`shubh_en_narration`, `en-IN`)
**Visuals:** local only — matplotlib charts + inline-SVG illustrations, HyperFrames render

---

## Scene 1 — hook (opening)

> Right now, you are made of about thirty seven trillion cells. And every single one of them is mostly water.

**Visual:** human silhouette assembled from a drifting particle field, slow push-in.

## Scene 2 — water (narration)

> About sixty percent of your body is water. It is the stage where almost every chemical reaction inside you happens.

**Visual:** donut chart — water 60 % against the other constituents.

## Scene 3 — the full composition (narration)

> But water is only the beginning. The rest of you is protein, fat, mineral, and a trace of carbohydrate.

**Visual:** stacked bar breaking the body down into its five constituents.

## Scene 4 — protein and fat (narration)

> Proteins build and repair you, from antibodies to enzymes. Fats store your energy, insulate your organs, and form your cell membranes.

**Visual:** two panel chart — protein vs fat with their jobs listed.

## Scene 5 — minerals (narration)

> Minerals do the fine work. Calcium builds your bones, iron carries oxygen in your blood, and sodium and potassium fire every nerve impulse.

**Visual:** element-mass bar chart with three mineral call-outs.

## Scene 6 — inside one cell (narration)

> Now zoom into a single cell. A nucleus holds your DNA, ribosomes build your proteins, and mitochondria turn the food you eat into energy you can spend.

**Visual:** cell cross-section — nucleus, ribosomes, mitochondria with ATP sparks.

## Scene 7 — the answer (closing)

> Zoom out again, and it all comes down to six elements. Oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, calcium, and phosphorus. That is what you are made of.

**Visual:** six element chips rise into place under a final title.
