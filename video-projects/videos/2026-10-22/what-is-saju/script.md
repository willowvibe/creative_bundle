# What is Korean Saju? — script v1

- **Format:** 1080×1920, 50–60 s, Instagram Reels / TikTok
- **Voice:** Sarvam Bulbul v4 Flash (`en-IN`). No music.
- **Captions:** karaoke, inside the safe area.
- **Brand:** `presets/brands/saju-2026-07-03.json` palette and fonts.
- **CTA:** a soft, single mention of **cosmicsaju.com** in the last scene only.
  There is no "link in bio", no "sign up", and no logo before the end.

Word count: about 120 words. At Sarvam's pace plus scene tails, that runs about
52–56 s.

## How the CTA stays subtle

- The narration names the product once, as an answer to a question the
  viewer is already asking. It is not an instruction.
- The URL appears only on the end card, small, in the brand's secondary colour,
  for the last ~3 s. It stays inside the safe area, so the TikTok UI does not
  cover it.
- Platform captions (post text) can carry the link. On-video stays soft.
