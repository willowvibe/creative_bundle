## Why the closing line is the CTA

The reference short answers a question ("what do your eight characters say?")
and lets the URL do the rest. This one ends on the question "which one are
you?", and the CTA is the answer to it — spoken, not shouted, with the end card
holding the URL for the whole CTA line plus a 1.2 s tail. No "link in bio", no
logo, no second mention.
