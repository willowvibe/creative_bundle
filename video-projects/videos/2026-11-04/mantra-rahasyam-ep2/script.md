# మంత్ర రహస్యం — Episode 2: శ్రీశైలం యాత్ర (The Srisailam Pilgrimage)

**Gate 1: script, v2** (2026-11-04: viewer-hook pass; v1 kept as `script.v1.md`). The dialogue gate comes next.
Telugu, 16:9, English .srt. Target **≈ 9:35** (Ep1 was 5:17).
Look: **bright, natural daylight everywhere**: sunny home, green Nallamala ghat road, Srisailam temple in
morning sun. Yamaloka is a **golden court with white pillars and soft divine light**, never dark.
Featured mantra: **ఓం నమః శివాయ** (Panchakshari). The channel's Mahamrityunjaya chant returns at the temple.

**What v2 adds** (no dialogue line changed; all story changes are marked **[v2]** and listed under "Open for
review"): a question map that keeps the viewer leaning in, a shot plan with directed camera moves (Uni3C), a look
plan, a sound cue sheet, pacing targets, and the thumbnail and Shorts moments chosen up front.

## Series engine introduced in this episode
- **Chitragupta's ledger:** "A life returned must save 108 lives." The girl Abhi saved in Ep1 is already written
  as **1**. 107 remain.
- **The counter:** a golden numeral only Abhi sees, rising each time a life is saved. Drawn as a **HyperFrames
  overlay** (same font, glow and chime every episode), never generated inside the AI video.
- **The manuscript:** Tatayya was a keeper of an ancient palm-leaf manuscript, **మంత్ర రహస్యం**, holding 108 mantras
  like the beads of a japa mala. Only Tatayya's rudraksha opens the way to it.
- **The villain:** a stranger working for an unseen **"Guruji"** who also wants the manuscript.

The audience learns the 108 rule in Yamaloka (scene 4) before Abhi does. Abhi only sees numbers; he works out
that they count lives at the ghat (scene 7), and learns what 108 means from the priest (scene 9). So he keeps it
from his parents without having to decide to.

## What keeps the viewer watching

Intrigue is information held back. Each scene either **opens a question** or **pays one off**, and the episode
never goes more than about 90 s without one or the other.

| Loop | Question the viewer carries | Opened | Paid off |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | The car hanging over the edge: does the last person get out? | 0:00 cold open (cut away before we know) | 6:40, sc. 7 |
| L2 | Shiva: "others you must save". Who, and how many? | 0:30 previously | 3:30 the audience learns 108; 6:40 Abhi learns the numbers are lives |
| L3 | What is the golden number only Abhi sees? | 3:45 sc. 4 match cut | 6:40 lives; **reopened** 8:30 with "108", carried to Ep3 |
| L4 | Who is watching Abhi? | **[v2]** 1:20 black car outside the house | sightings at 4:45 dhaba, 7:30 temple gate, 9:00 phone call, 9:15 Guruji's hands; **carried to Ep3** |
| L5 | Why did Tatayya go to Srisailam alone every year? | 4:15 terrace | 8:10 the priest |
| L6 | Where is the manuscript, and how does the rudraksha open the way? | 8:20 priest | **carried to Ep3** |

**Dramatic irony:** the audience learns the 108 rule in Yamaloka (3:30). Abhi doesn't understand it until the
priest says "108" (8:30). For five minutes the viewer knows more than the hero, so every number he sees is a payoff
for them and a mystery for him.

## Dialogue draft

Emotion tags are for the ElevenLabs take; the narrator stays at ~30%. **Telugu is a first draft for Daddy's
review** at the dialogue gate.

**1. Previously**
- **Narrator** (warm, measured): గత భాగంలో... ఒక పసిపాప ప్రాణం కాపాడి మృత్యువు ఒడిలోకి వెళ్ళిన అభినవ్... తండ్రి జపించిన మృత్యుంజయ మంత్రంతో, శివయ్య దయతో... తిరిగి వచ్చాడు.
  *Previously... Abhinav saved a little girl and fell into death's lap... and through the Mrityunjaya mantra his father chanted, and Shiva's grace... he came back.*
- **Lord Shiva** (echo, **Ep1 audio reused**): అభినవ్... నీ ప్రాణం నీ ఒక్కడిది కాదు. నువ్వు కాపాడాల్సిన వాళ్ళు ఇంకా ఉన్నారు.
  *Abhinav... your life is not yours alone. There are still others you must save.*

**2. Home, three weeks later**
- **Lakshmi** (fussing, loving): నెమ్మదిగా రా కన్నా... డాక్టర్ గారు ఇంకా జాగ్రత్తగా ఉండమన్నారు.
  *Slowly, dear... the doctor said you must still be careful.*
- **Abhinav** (smiling, teasing): అమ్మా, మూడు వారాలైంది. ఇంకా నన్ను చిన్నపిల్లాడిలా చూస్తావా?
  *Amma, it's been three weeks. Are you still going to treat me like a little kid?*
- **Raghava** (gentle, pleased): డాక్టర్ గారు ప్రయాణానికి ఒప్పుకున్నారు రా. ఇక నీ మాట నిలబెట్టుకునే సమయం వచ్చింది.
  *The doctor has agreed you can travel. Now it's time to keep your word.*
- **Abhinav** (resolute, warm): శ్రీశైలం. ఈ ఆదివారమే. తాతయ్య కోరిక... ఈసారి ఏ అడ్డూ రాదు.
  *Srisailam. This very Sunday. Tatayya's wish... this time nothing will stop it.*
- **Lakshmi** (emotional, devout): నేను కూడా ఒక మొక్కు మొక్కుకున్నాను రా... నువ్వు కళ్ళు తెరిస్తే, పాతాళగంగ మెట్లు చెప్పులు లేకుండా నడుస్తానని.
  *I made a vow too... that if you opened your eyes, I would walk the Pathala Ganga steps barefoot.*
- **Abhinav** (touched): అమ్మా...
  *Amma...*

**3. Chinni visits**
- **Sujatha** (hesitant, grateful): నమస్తే అండీ... మేము గుర్తున్నామా? ఆ రోజు రోడ్డు మీద... మా చిన్ని...
  *Namaste... do you remember us? That day on the road... our Chinni...*
- **Lakshmi** (recognising, warm): అయ్యో, రండమ్మా... లోపలికి రండి!
  *Oh, come in, come inside!*
- **Sujatha** (tearful): మీ అబ్బాయి లేకపోతే... ఈ రోజు నా బిడ్డ నా దగ్గర ఉండేది కాదు. ఈ రుణం ఏ జన్మలో తీర్చుకోవాలో...
  *If not for your son... my child wouldn't be with me today. In which lifetime will I repay this debt...*
- **Chinni** (shy, sweet): అన్నయ్యా... ఇది నీకోసం. నేనే గీశా.
  *Big brother... this is for you. I drew it myself.*
- **Abhinav** (soft laugh, moved): నేనా? సూపర్ హీరోలా ఉన్నానే!
  *That's me? I look like a superhero!*
- **Chinni** (innocent, certain): నువ్వు నిజంగానే సూపర్ హీరోవి అన్నయ్యా!
  *You really are a superhero, big brother!*

**4. Yamaloka**
- **Narrator** (hushed, mysterious): అదే క్షణం... కాలానికి అతీతమైన యమలోకంలో...
  *In that same moment... in Yamaloka, beyond time...*
- **Yama Dhootha 1** (troubled, bowing): చిత్రగుప్తా... అభినవ్ ప్రాణాన్ని తీసుకురాలేకపోయాం. మహాదేవుడే స్వయంగా అడ్డుపడ్డాడు.
  *Chitragupta... we could not bring Abhinav's soul. Mahadeva himself stood in our way.*
- **Yama Dhootha 2** (puzzled): కానీ మీ లెక్కల్లో... అతని ఆయుష్షు ఆ రోజే ముగిసింది కదా ప్రభూ?
  *But in your accounts... his lifespan ended that very day, didn't it, lord?*
- **Chitragupta** (calm, grave, turning pages): నిజమే. కానీ శివాజ్ఞకు ఎదురు లేదు. చూడండి... చిత్రగుప్తుడి లెక్క తప్పింది.
  *True. But no one defies Shiva's command. Look... Chitragupta's ledger is unbalanced.*
- **Chitragupta** (deliberate, resonant): తిరిగి ఇచ్చిన ప్రాణం... నూట ఎనిమిది ప్రాణాలను కాపాడాలి.
  *A life returned... must save one hundred and eight lives.*
- **Yama Dhootha 1** (stunned): నూట ఎనిమిది?!
  *One hundred and eight?!*
- **Chitragupta** (softening, writing): ఒకటి ఇప్పటికే రాసి ఉంది... రోడ్డు మీద అతను కాపాడిన ఆ పసిపాప. ఇంకా నూట ఏడు మిగిలి ఉన్నాయి.
  *One is already written... the little girl he saved on the road. One hundred and seven remain.*
- **Chitragupta** (mysterious, final): ప్రతి సంఖ్యను అతను తన కళ్ళతో చూస్తాడు. కానీ దాని అర్థం... అతనే తెలుసుకోవాలి.
  *He will see every number with his own eyes. But its meaning... he must discover for himself.*

*(stylus writes **1** → match cut to earth, the same **1** before Abhi's eyes)*

- **Chinni** (curious): అన్నయ్యా... ఏమైంది? ఏం చూస్తున్నావు?
  *Big brother... what happened? What are you looking at?*

**5. Terrace, night**

## Continuity chains and transitions
Ep1 played as separate cut scenes because every shot was built from its own still. Ep2 rules:

- **Location plate per scene:** first generate one empty set image (car interior, ghat hairpin, house hall,
  Yamaloka court, temple courtyard, priest's mandapam). Every still in that scene uses it as a reference, next to
  the character refs. Each plate also gets a continuity sheet: who sits where, rudraksha on the right wrist, sun
  direction, time of day.
- **Coverage:** each scene opens on a wide that sets the geography, then cuts inside it. The camera stays on one
  side of the action (180° rule).
- **Keyframe pairs inside a chain:** shot N's end still is the same moment as shot N+1's start still, seen from the
  new angle (a cut on action). Clips are made from first + last frame (Wan 2.2 FLF2V; needs a new `rpc` workflow),
  so both ends are approved stills and faces can't drift. Fallback: last-frame chaining, re-anchored every 2–3 clips.
- **Sound bridges:** one ambience bed per scene, and J/L cuts (the next line or sound starts before the picture cuts).

**Chains** (links are the order of keyframes; each arrow is one FLF clip):
- **A, Chinni's hug (sc. 3 → 4):** Chinni runs in → arms around Abhi → his face over her shoulder, time slows →
  frame floods gold → (gen transition) gold resolves into the Yamaloka court.

## Look

- **Bright daylight stays the rule.** Contrast comes from light direction, not darkness: low morning sun, side and
  rim light on faces, shafts through the Nallamala trees, lamp glow inside the temple.
- **Light with a source:** every interior has a visible window, lamp or diya that the light comes from. Flat, even
  light is what makes AI frames look fake.
- **Palette per scene:**
  - home: warm white
  - Yamaloka: gold and white, haze, bloom on highlights
  - ghat: green, hard sun, slightly cooler and contrastier once the brakes fail
  - temple: saffron and stone gold
  - flashback: warm, slightly faded, soft vignette, more grain
  - Guruji: one warm lamp source, still bright enough to read
- **Series film-look pass** on the whole cut, as one preset reused every episode:
  - fine grain, gentle halation on highlights and a light vignette
  - one LUT for the series
  - natural skin; no oversaturation
- **Depth of field:** close-ups shallow; inserts (rudraksha, ledger, photograph) macro.
- **Counter:** the same golden numeral, glow and position logic as listed under production notes, every episode.
