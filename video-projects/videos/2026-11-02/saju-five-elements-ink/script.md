# The Five Elements: ink-wash test short

Cosmic Saju series test of the Lemo-Opuscar **Chinese Ink Wash** style
(github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar, `styles/ink-wash`, MIT engine). Every frame
is drawn in code, so no image or video model is used. 1080×1920 at 30 fps, target about 40 s.

Structure: **a hanging scroll.** One vertical painting is built up one element at a time,
and the camera tilts down through it. The only colour is vermilion: it is the Fire
ember and, at the end, the seal on the end card.
