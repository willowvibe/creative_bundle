For DeveeKitchen map Shorts the owner asked for (2026-11-26, Andhra district-food Short):
- a large photo card per dish rather than photos only inside small district shapes;
- the map placed so the named district is never behind the card (map fitted in the band below it);
- the map zooming to each district as it is named ("zoom with map drawing looks good, rather than a static map");
- no burned captions (`captions: {mode: sidecar}`, SRT kept for YouTube CC);
- "more interesting": numbered countdown cards, a pop per card, a quick photo teaser at the start;
- closing line in natural Telugu pointing to the next part ("వచ్చే వీడియోలో తెలంగాణ చూద్దాం").

**Why:** a static map with small photos and lots of empty sea looked dull for a Short.
**How to apply:** start every new map Short (Telangana next) from this shape; recipe in `video-editor/docs/MAP_SHORTS.md`. Related: [[deveekitchen-slate-trend-scan]].
